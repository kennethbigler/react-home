# Slam Bidding (SAYC)

Slam bidding is about **controls** (aces, kings, voids, singletons) more than raw
points. Two partners with a fit and ~33 combined points consider 6; ~37 consider
7. But you don't bid slam off two cashing aces — so you ask.

## 1. When to think about slam
- A known fit plus ~31+ combined points, OR a fit plus useful shortness/controls.
- Triggers: a strong jump shift, a splinter, Jacoby 2NT with a slam-positive
  reply, a quantitative 4NT, a 2♣ opening with a positive response.
- Before launching an ace-ask, make sure you control the **first two rounds** of
  every side suit (you don't want to be off two cashing tricks in one suit). If a
  side suit is unguarded, **cue-bid** to find controls rather than blasting 4NT.

## 2. Blackwood (4NT) — standard (ace-asking)
- **4NT** (when not quantitative — i.e. with an agreed trump suit) asks for aces:
  - 5♣ = 0 or 4 aces
  - 5♦ = 1 ace
  - 5♥ = 2 aces
  - 5♠ = 3 aces
- Follow with **5NT** to ask for kings (same step scheme: 6♣=0/4, 6♦=1, …).
- Only the **partnership** value matters: if two aces are missing, sign off at 5.
- **Caution:** 4NT is quantitative (not Blackwood) when no suit is agreed and the
  auction has been natural notrump — see §7. Context decides.

## 3. Roman Key Card Blackwood (RKCB) — the modern default many add
RKCB treats the **king and queen of the agreed trump suit** as extra "key cards."
There are **5 key cards**: the 4 aces + the king of trumps. Two response schemes;
most pairs play **1430**:

**RKCB 1430 responses to 4NT:**
- 5♣ = **1** or **4** key cards
- 5♦ = **0** or **3** key cards
- 5♥ = **2** or 5 key cards **without** the trump queen
- 5♠ = **2** or 5 key cards **with** the trump queen

**RKCB 0314 responses (the alternative ordering):**
- 5♣ = **0** or **3**
- 5♦ = **1** or **4**
- 5♥ = 2 (or 5) without the queen
- 5♠ = 2 (or 5) with the queen

- After a 5♣/5♦ key-card reply, the **next step up (cheapest bid that isn't the
  trump suit)** asks for the **trump queen**; replies: cheapest = "no queen,"
  next steps show the queen and a side king.
- **5NT** then asks for kings (number of outside kings), inviting a grand slam.
- **Voids:** with a void and an even/odd key-card count, jump to the 6-level
  showing key cards plus a void (partnership agreement on exact steps).
- SAYC's *pure* default is plain Blackwood; **note RKCB 1430 as the common
  expert add-on** and ask which the convention card uses if it matters.

## 4. Gerber (4♣)
- A **direct jump to 4♣ over a natural NT bid (1NT/2NT)** asks for **aces**:
  - 4♦ = 0 or 4, 4♥ = 1, 4♠ = 2, 4NT = 3 aces.
  - Follow-up **5♣** asks for kings by the same steps.
- Gerber is used over **notrump** because 4NT there is quantitative, not ace-
  asking. Over a suit fit, use Blackwood/RKCB, not Gerber.

## 5. Control bids (cue bids)
- Once a trump suit is agreed and the partnership is in a game-force, bidding a
  **new suit at the 4-level** below game shows a **first-round control** (ace or
  void) in that suit and slam interest, inviting partner to cue back.
- Cue bids go "up the line" (cheapest control first). Skipping a suit denies a
  control there. Use cue bids when you have shortness/voids that Blackwood can't
  evaluate, or to check a specific suit's control before committing.
- Second-round controls (king/singleton) are typically shown on the second round
  of cue-bidding.

## 6. Grand Slam Force (5NT)
- A jump to **5NT** (Josephine, when not preceded by 4NT) asks partner to bid a
  grand slam in the agreed trump suit **with two of the top three trump honors**,
  otherwise sign off at 6. Used when controls are known and only trump quality is
  in question.

## 7. Quantitative slam tries in notrump
- **1NT–4NT** / **2NT–4NT**: quantitative, "bid 6NT if maximum, pass if
  minimum." Not ace-asking.
- **1NT–5NT**: invites a grand slam, forces to at least 6NT, asks opener to bid
  6NT (minimum) or 7NT (maximum).
- To ask for aces over notrump, use **Gerber (4♣)**, not 4NT.

## 8. Choosing the right tool — quick guide
| Situation | Tool |
|-----------|------|
| Trump fit agreed, count aces/key cards | Blackwood or RKCB (4NT) |
| Notrump auction, count aces | Gerber (4♣) |
| Notrump auction, invite slam on points | Quantitative 4NT / 5NT |
| Fit + shortness, need to locate controls | Cue bids |
| Controls known, only trump honors in doubt | Grand Slam Force (5NT) |
