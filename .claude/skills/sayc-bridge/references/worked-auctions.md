# Worked Auction Examples (SAYC) — full annotated sequences

Complete bidding sequences from start to finish, annotated bid-by-bid. Use these
to pattern-match real auctions. Notation: bids are listed in order; "—" marks
your side, opponents in (parentheses). P = Pass, X = double, XX = redouble.

## 1. Uncontested constructive auctions

**1.1 — Simple major fit, stop in part-score**
`1♥ – 2♥ – P`
Opener 12–15, responder 6–10 with 3+ hearts (single raise). Opener has no extras,
passes. Final contract 2♥.

**1.2 — Limit raise accepted to game**
`1♠ – 3♠ – 4♠`
3♠ = limit raise (10–12, 4 trumps, invitational). Opener has ~15+ and accepts,
bidding game.

**1.3 — Forcing 1NT then preference**
`1♥ – 1NT – 2♣ – 2♥`
1NT forcing (6–12, no fit). Opener shows a second suit (clubs). Responder takes a
preference to 2♥ with a doubleton heart and a minimum — back to the 5-2 fit.

**1.4 — Reverse showing extras**
`1♦ – 1♠ – 2♥ – 2NT`
2♥ is a reverse (17+, 5+ diamonds / 4 hearts, forcing). Responder, with a minimum
and a club stopper, bids 2NT. Opener now clarifies (raise NT with extras, etc.).

**1.5 — Fourth-suit forcing to find the strain**
`1♣ – 1♥ – 1♠ – 2♦ – 2NT – 3NT`
2♦ = fourth-suit forcing (artificial, GF). Opener shows a diamond stopper with
2NT. Responder raises to game. Final 3NT.

**1.6 — New minor forcing to find the 5-3 fit**
`1♦ – 1♥ – 1NT – 2♣ – 2♥ – 4♥`
1NT rebid = 12–14 balanced. 2♣ = new minor forcing (checkback). Opener shows
3-card heart support (2♥). Responder, game-forcing with 5 hearts, bids 4♥ in the
5-3 fit.

## 2. Notrump auctions

**2.1 — Stayman, no fit, to game**
`1NT – 2♣ – 2♦ – 3NT`
Stayman; opener denies a 4-card major (2♦); responder had game values and bids
3NT.

**2.2 — Stayman, heart fit, game**
`1NT – 2♣ – 2♥ – 4♥`
Opener shows 4 hearts; responder has 4 hearts and game values → 4♥.

**2.3 — Jacoby transfer, invitational**
`1NT – 2♦ – 2♥ – 2NT – 3NT`
Transfer to hearts; responder's 2NT = invite with exactly 5 hearts. Opener,
maximum, chooses 3NT (with a doubleton heart) — could bid 4♥ with 3.

**2.4 — Texas transfer to game**
`1NT – 4♦ – 4♥ – P`
Texas: 6+ hearts, game values, no slam interest. Opener completes to 4♥, declares.

**2.5 — Smolen**
`1NT – 2♣ – 2♦ – 3♥ – 4♠`
Stayman, no major (2♦), Smolen 3♥ = 4 hearts + 5 spades GF. Opener has 3 spades,
bids 4♠ (5-3 fit, strong hand declares).

**2.6 — Quantitative, declined**
`1NT – 4NT – P`
4NT quantitative (not Blackwood). Opener has a minimum (15) and passes.

## 3. Slam auctions

**3.1 — Jacoby 2NT into slam via shortness**
`1♠ – 2NT – 3♥ – 4♣ – 4NT – 5♥ – 6♠`
2NT = Jacoby (GF spade raise). 3♥ = singleton/void heart. 4♣ = cue (club control)
showing slam interest opposite the working values. 4NT = RKC. 5♥ = 2 key cards
without the trump queen. Opener places 6♠.

**3.2 — RKC Blackwood, stop at 5**
`1♥ – 3♥ – 4NT – 5♦ – 5♥`
Limit-ish raise context; 4NT = RKC (1430). 5♦ = 0 or 3 key cards → here it's 0/1
short of slam, so asker signs off in 5♥.

**3.3 — Gerber over 1NT**
`1NT – 4♣ – 4♥ – 6NT`
4♣ = Gerber (ace ask over NT). 4♥ = 1 ace. With the count for slam and only 1 ace
missing, asker bids 6NT.

**3.4 — Splinter slam try**
`1♠ – 4♦ – 4♠`
4♦ = splinter (GF spade raise, diamond shortness). Opener has wasted diamond
honors (e.g. ♦Qx) and no extras, signs off in 4♠. (With working values, opener
would cue-bid toward slam instead.)

## 4. Competitive auctions

**4.1 — Negative double finds the fit**
`1♣ – (1♠) – X – (P) – 2♥`
X = negative double showing 4 hearts and values. Opener bids the heart fit.

**4.2 — Takeout double, responder forced to bid**
`(1♥) – X – (P) – 1♠`
X = takeout (opening values, support for unbid suits). Advancer bids best suit,
1♠, even with 0–8.

**4.3 — Strong hand: double then bid**
`(1♥) – X – (P) – 2♣ – (P) – 2♠`
Doubler then bids spades = too strong to overcall (~17+), showing the spade suit
and big values.

**4.4 — Michaels and the 2NT ask**
`(1♥) – 2♥ – (P) – 2NT – (P) – 3♦`
2♥ = Michaels (spades + a minor, 5-5). 2NT asks the minor; opener shows diamonds.

**4.5 — Cue-bid raise of an overcall**
`(1♦) – 1♠ – (P) – 2♦ – (P) – 2♠`
2♦ (cue of opener's suit) = a real limit-raise-or-better in spades. Overcaller
with a minimum signs off in 2♠.

**4.6 — Competing by the Law**
`1♠ – (2♥) – 2♠ – (3♥) – 3♠`
9-card spade fit → the Law sanctions competing to the 3-level. Responder's 2♠ was
competitive; over 3♥, the partnership pushes to 3♠.

## 5. Preemptive auctions

**5.1 — Weak two and the 2NT ask (Ogust)**
`2♥ – 2NT – 3♠ – 4♥`
2♥ weak two. 2NT = forcing inquiry. 3♠ (Ogust) = good hand, good suit (maximum).
Asker bids game.

**5.2 — Preempt raising the preempt (Law)**
`(P) – 3♣ – (P) – 5♣` *(by a strong responder)* or
`2♠ – 4♠`
A direct raise of partner's weak two/preempt to game is shape/Law-based, often
"advance sacrifice" or "they're making something" — not a slam try.

**5.3 — Gambling 3NT, partner pulls**
`3NT – 4♣`
3NT = gambling (solid 7-card minor). Responder lacks outside stoppers and pulls
to 4♣ ("pass or correct"); opener passes if clubs is the suit, corrects to 4♦ if
diamonds.

**5.4 — Strong 2♣ to game**
`2♣ – 2♦ – 2♠ – 3♠ – 4♠`
2♣ strong/GF. 2♦ waiting. 2♠ natural GF suit. 3♠ raise. 4♠ game (slam explored if
extras). The auction cannot stop below game.
